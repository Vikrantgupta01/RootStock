Urgency: How soon the household needs help.
Used for Case.urgency.
- LOW: Help can wait for the next regular visit.
- MEDIUM: Help is needed within a week.
- HIGH: Risk to safety or basic needs within 48 hours.

NeedCategory: What a household needs help with.
Used for Need.category.
- FOOD: Food parcels or supermarket vouchers. Also said as: groceries, food hamper, nothing in the fridge.
- ENERGY_BILL: Help with gas or electricity bills, including disconnection notices. Also said as: power bill, electricity cut off, gas bill.
- RENT: Help with rent arrears or bond. Also said as: behind on rent, eviction notice, bond.
